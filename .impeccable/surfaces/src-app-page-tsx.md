# Dawn home

Mode: operate

Audience: Kyle and the Artometrics team, on a phone. Job: run the village, approve what leaves, keep quests, walk the campus day. Action: open a section, search, add a quest, talk to an agent, walk the next stop.

Constraints: keep village, approvals, group chat, quests, team card, geolocation map, Cardinal Quest, `/atlas?embed=1`, and `/studio`. Mobile first. No new claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Dawn on a phone is a calm night list, not an espresso pixel dashboard. The first screen shows the work (quests, the village, the walk) and refuses a marketing hero.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground, warm grey ink in three tiers, clay accent only on the open section’s mark, a waiting approval, and focus. White pill for the single primary action. Serif wordmark, the existing grotesque for UI. Atlas is its own night: charcoal ground, lighter roads, neon only on the path, the live zone, and the goal, dark-blue pill markers, the pixel walker.

STORY: You see what is waiting, open an agent or a quest, and walk the day. Nothing leaves until you approve.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Portrait phone. Round menu left, centered screen title, round You button right. Quest rows are a title and a muted relative date, no cards. A white “+ New quest” pill sits above a search field pinned to the bottom safe area. The drawer is closed. On Atlas the same corners float over a full-bleed map, the walker already on the neon path, a translucent search on top, round layers and recenter controls on the right, and a dark sheet with filter chips.

FORM: Brief-pinned Claude and Grok night shell. Seed 0a035e3d assigned the fourth grounded direction, a pocket day timetable. Its goldenrod bed and raked slides conflict with the pinned night, so they are translated away. What remains is one row size, rank by weight, and state as a mark in a fixed cell (filled done, hollow open). Acetate-tab raise: the clay accent fills only the control of the open section. Vertical-feed raise: one white pill is the only filled action on a screen.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Signature interaction: dragging the Atlas sheet and watching minor labels fade as you zoom, while the pixel character steps along the neon path.

Unresolved: Library holds agents, the room, and the team card. Documents beyond that are later.
