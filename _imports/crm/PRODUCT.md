# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind. Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Firebase Hosting, optional Firebase AI Logic. The user named this stack. Inferred: no extra backend, database, or component brand beyond that.

## Users

Kyle, solo founder of Artometrics (apps, magazines, YouTube, client work). Shared desk with team@artometrics.com. Inferred: he uses it on iPhone Safari, often away from a laptop, and adds people in the moment.

## Product Purpose

A private CRM for the people, companies, deals, notes, and follow-ups of Artometrics. Success means he can open it from the home screen, find a person, see the money and the next step, and write it down without waiting on the network.

## Positioning

One allowlisted desk for a studio that sells apps, magazines, video, and client work. Not a general sales suite. The pipeline is five stages and the home screen is today.

## Operating Context

Phone-first, including add-to-home-screen. Contacts arrive from Instagram, referrals, and client work. Follow-ups have due dates. Files sit on a contact. A model may summarize history and draft a follow-up; it never sends.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed: Google sign-in, Sign in with Apple wired and ready, persistent sessions, Firestore, Storage attachments, rules limited to team@artometrics.com and kylesmcauliffe@gmail.com, contacts, companies, deal kanban (Lead, Contacted, Proposal, Won, Lost) with value, notes and activity, tasks with a Today view, global search, CSV import/export, phone quick-add, demo data toggle, PWA, optimistic writes, Firestore offline persistence.

Inferred and undecided until deploy: Firebase project crm-510519 may still need Auth, Firestore, Storage, a web app, and Apple provider setup in the console. AI stays off until App Check and Firebase AI Logic are enabled. No billing, hosting site, or Apple key is in this repo.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Artometrics. Voice: few words. Icon-first. Dark near-black background, cream text, signal red around #E10600, Tiffany blue secondary, Anton for display headings.

## Evidence on Hand

No customer list, prices, or testimonials were provided. Demo records are synthetic and labeled demo. Do not present them as real clients.

## Product Principles

- The phone is the main desk. Large targets, instant local writes, a session that stays.
- The allowlist is the lock. Everyone else sees nothing.
- Today comes before the archive.
- A draft is not a send.
- Demo data is obvious and removable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Inferred: iPhone Safari, one-handed use, readable type at arm's length, contrast that holds on an OLED phone outdoors, and controls that work with VoiceOver labels.
