# Credits — Pantheon Forge

The body, hair, clothes, weapons, banner, face paintings, brush overlay, hall, and floor are drawn in code for this prototype. They are original. No mesh or texture was downloaded.

## Not included

- No Quaternius, Kenney, Mixamo, or Meshy files. The game already ships some of those under `site/` (see the repo `CREDITS.md` and `ASSETS.md`). Forge does not copy them.
- No photograph of a person, and no crop of a painting. Joan of Arc, Napoleon Bonaparte, and Sun Tzu are named as historical subjects. Their presets are period clothes on the shared rig: plate and banner, greatcoat and bicorne, robe and straight sword. Proportions stay in the adult range of the height slider. Napoleon is not drawn short.

## Software

- [three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js) — MIT. The desk imports it from npm. The outline and the painterly ramp are small shader hooks on `MeshToonMaterial`, not a copied shader file.
- [Vite](https://github.com/vitejs/vite) — MIT. Dev server and `npm run build` only.

No paid API, no account, and no secret is required to run or build the desk.
